## Two decisions that shape the rest

1. **An embedding of private text is private data.** Vectors are semantically
   invertible. No-Echo extends to the vector layer. The embedder sits
   **downstream** of the Disclosure Evaluator and embeds sealed envelopes only.

   `public-map.db` (c.vStore) is born public and may travel with the repo.
   `private-graph.db` is gitignored, local-only, never in a repo, never in
   cloud sync. A public-lane agent has **no path to the file**.
